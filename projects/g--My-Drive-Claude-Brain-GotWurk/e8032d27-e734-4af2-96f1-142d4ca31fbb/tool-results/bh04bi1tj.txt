11-Applies to every role below, no exceptions. This is what "GotWurk aligned" means in practice:

16-4. **Nothing fabricated.** No invented client logos, testimonials, case studies, credentials, or "connected" integrations that aren't real. If something isn't built yet, say so — this is a standing site/product principle, extend it to every client and internal conversation.
17-5. **Client-facing tone:** direct, no fluff, no bullet points in client messages, no emojis, no em dashes, short paragraphs.
18-6. **Keep the record straight.** Log meaningful decisions in [decisions/log.md](../decisions/log.md) as they're made. Update [GOTWURK_OS.md](../GOTWURK_OS.md) and [brain/00-README.md](../brain/00-README.md) in the same session as any change to positioning, methodology, the active offer, pricing, folder structure, or this file — not after the fact.
19-7. **Social media is an upsell, never a headline.** Sold only once an audit surfaces a real Growth-side gap, layered onto an existing engagement.
20-8. **Surface gaps, don't paper over them.** If something referenced elsewhere in the vault is actually retired, unbuilt, or undecided (e.g. the 90-day offer gap flagged in `brain/00-README.md`), say so plainly instead of working around it quietly.
