As of 2026-10-06 Willem is focusing heavily on e-commerce alongside the café. The website must match the café: grey, concrete, minimalist, brutalist, with metallic modern silver accents. Products: cowboy hats, boots and real leather goods, very few, to create scarcity and exclusivity. Products carry no logo on the outside (he calls that tacky); branding is hidden inside or underneath.

Brand taste rules he stated: no pink anywhere, nothing Toy Story or cowboy-cliche in copy (no "howdy", "yeehaw"), no menu or prices in the brand kit, no window signage slide, no "Flat white R42" style lines. He likes the concrete slab cover, the glazed corner slide and the logo as is.

**Why:** these were direct corrections to the brand kit PDF. The shop is not in the financial model yet.

**How to apply:** keep any brand, web or copy work in that register. Treat the online shop as a new revenue line that still needs modelling and pricing before any figures are shown. See [[feedback-rejected-renders-erased]].
